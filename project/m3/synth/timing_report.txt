ROOT CAUSE OF SETUP VIOLATION
Post-routing critical path is ~9.0 ns (vs. 3.33 ns budget) because:

  1. OpenROAD resizer remapped the adder tree from Yosys/ABC's 3-input
     xnor3_1 / xor3_1 cells (~7 levels) into 2-input xnor2_2 / xor2_2
     cells (~16 levels). This is a different optimization pass.
  2. The 350 µm standalone die adds 30–40 µm wire delays per net.
     Cumulative wire delay across 16 levels: ~5–8 ns.

The pre-P&R ABC estimate of 2.1–2.3 ns assumed min-load Liberty arcs and
ABC's preferred 3-input cell mapping. Neither holds in OpenLane's flow.
